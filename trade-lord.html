What this training does and does not know

Lessons and trade observations build a saved research memory. Model weights are not retrained. A trader’s private reasoning, other wallets and unseen market information remain unknown. There is no profitability or speed guarantee.

Trade Lord looks for four things in an answer:

  • What actually happened
  • What the pattern might mean
  • What the data cannot tell us
  • What we should test next

Evidence mode answers questions from saved statistics and lessons. Open-ended conversation requires a connected language model.
