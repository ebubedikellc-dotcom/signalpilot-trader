How this forward demo works

A passing grade means the numeric decision followed the programmed rules. A profitable simulation can still contain a bad decision. A sound decision can lose.

The feed supplies real timestamped bid/ask prices and completed candles. Future prices are unknown. Fills are simulated using fresh observations after the decision, with declared fees, adverse fills and liquidity limits. Fifteen-second sampling can miss moves between observations; it cannot duplicate real exchange execution. Feedback is shown here for owner review; it is not sent back to the AI or saved as a lesson. This does not retrain the model.

Connected scope: Kraken DOGE/USD, long cash positions only. Gold, stocks, options and other tokens are unavailable until their specific feeds and execution models are verified. Rules stay fixed during each run. No account, wallet or real-order connection exists.

Fixed experiment rules

Buy only after a completed five-minute close above the previous candle’s high. Planned stop: 1.5% below the frozen entry; target: 5% above. Maximum planned risk: $25 and 0.5% of virtual equity. Maximum committed cash: $1,000. Costs must leave net reward/risk of at least 1.5.

Simulated commission: 0.4% of the frozen entry price per token on each side. Adverse price adjustment: 0.1%. Use at most 10% of displayed best bid/ask quantity. One immediate entry attempt after the answer; expire the setup after twenty minutes. Observe exits for at most thirty minutes. These are fixed test rules, not an established profitable strategy.
